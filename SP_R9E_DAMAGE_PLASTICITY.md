## Implementation and limits
New separate uniaxial isothermal research kernel. Compression positive, no tension, effective stress yield with linear hardening and postpeak constant effective yield; scalar damage linear in accumulated postpeak plastic strain. Irreversible state and complete crushing retained on unload/reload; exact active set, pure trial, analytical tangent. No physical global failure assertion. Explicitly not Neuenschwander's calibrated model or SP/TKP law. For postpeak external work G_work, plastic softening length p_soft=2 G_work/(l fc). Total dissipated postpeak energy additionally includes released peak stored energy; report separately. No claim that varying transverse section fibre width is a legitimate localization length. Isothermal energy conservation check, monotonic path subdivision and cyclic work checks, inverse linear unloading slope diagnostic; monotonic data alone not identifiable. No thermal history transfer, LITS coupling, basic creep, fatigue, tensile damage, multiaxial effects or structural integration.
